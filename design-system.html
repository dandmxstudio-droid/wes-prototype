6.42 — WES-DRIVEN ADAPTATION

Wes-Driven Adaptation

Users should never feel like they are maintaining a profile or training an algorithm. They simply talk naturally with Wes. When meaningful new insights emerge, Wesembly adapts Today’s plan and What Wes knows — and clearly but subtly explains why.

THE MODEL
Talk to WesWes learns something meaningfulToday’s plan adaptsWhat Wes knows updates
Each step is conditional. A conversation only moves the chain forward when it produces an insight worth reflecting back. Downstream surfaces inherit the change — the user sees one explanation, at the source, not six notifications.
01Talk, don’t maintainThere is nothing to fill in, rate or keep up to date. The only input is a natural conversation with Wes — and the user can correct anything he got wrong.
02Only meaningful changeMost conversations change nothing visible. The system adapts when a new priority, interest, constraint or change of situation emerges — not on every message.
03Explain quietlyWhen something changes, say what changed and where it came from in one line of secondary text. Never celebrate it, count it or ask for more.
